DJ works from the field (phone, less organized than at his desk) and periodically wants a quick catch-up. When he asks for a **"status update"** — or any near-phrasing ("update me", "let's discuss what's still open", "where do we stand", "what have we been doing") — give a **condensed recap of just the last ~3–4 hours of THIS session**, not a long-term roadmap (he'll ask for that separately).

**Format:** short, grouped, one line per item. Three buckets:
- **✅ Shipped / done** — just "done, it's shipped." NOT how it was solved (that's not a status update — solved = solved).
- **⏳ Waiting on you** — the actionable items needing DJ's input/decision/tap. This is the most useful bucket — lead with or emphasize it.
- **🔨 Open / in progress** — being built by the other sessions, one line each.

**Why:** he's catching up fast between jobs; detail on solved problems is noise. He wants "what's done, what needs me, what's cooking." Keep it scannable and phone-friendly (spoken-friendly, minimal symbols) per [[feedback_spoken_friendly_responses]] and [[feedback_over_status_line]].

**How to apply:** pull from the recent conversation turns, not memory/long-term. Condense hard. Don't re-explain resolved items.

**OVERLAP IS FINE (DJ 2026-08-21):** consecutive status updates SHOULD overlap in time — it's a rolling window, not a hard non-overlapping cutoff. The SAME item may appear on two updates in a row if it's still recent or still open. When in doubt, REPEAT a still-open/recent item rather than drop it because it showed last time — better he sees an open item twice than misses it. Only stop showing something once it's clearly done AND has aged out of the recent window.
