DJ 2026-10-04, relaying Cheryl's concern + his own view, on timing of candidate communication:
- **Cheryl:** be careful about communicating after business hours - "doesn't feel right."
- **DJ:** communicate in a timely manner, and if they want a job they won't mind **weekend** communication - BUT the **late-night** sends shouldn't happen for us.

**Synthesis / rule:** Candidate reach-outs, replies, and call invites go out during **reasonable daytime hours (~8 AM-8 PM Pacific)**. **Weekends are fine.** **No late-night messaging** (roughly after 8 PM / before 8 AM PT).

**Why:** texting applicants at odd hours looks unprofessional and reflects on the brand. (The first reach-out batch went at ~12:52-1:09 AM because that's when we were working - exactly what prompted the concern. Candidates replied fine during the day, so no harm done, but don't repeat it.)

**How to apply:** When drafting/working OUTSIDE the window, do NOT send immediately. Either (a) use ZipRecruiter's **"Schedule"** button on the message composer to schedule the send for the next morning, or (b) hold the draft and send (or have DJ send) during the next reasonable window. Prefer scheduling so messages still go out "timely" without landing at 1 AM. Times to DJ stay Pacific (see [[feedback_times_in_pacific]]). Relates to [[feedback_reachout_no_overpromise]] and the ZipRecruiter hiring flow [[project_ziprecruiter_lead_hiring]].
