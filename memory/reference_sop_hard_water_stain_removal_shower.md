Dan's method for removing hard water stains from shower glass. Captured from Dan 2026-10-15; also stored in the app Knowledge Base (topic `sop_hard_water_stain_removal_shower_glass`, category `sop`) and emailed to DJ.

**Products/tools:** Lime-Away (lime/hard-water remover spray); soap solution (the soapy window-wash in the spray bottle — exact product TBC); strip washer (T-bar sleeve applicator); rough non-scratch scrub pad; squeegee; aerosol glass cleaner ("spray can" — likely Sprayway, TBC); two cloths (one to wipe, one DRY to buff).

**Steps:**
1. Spray Lime-Away all over the glass.
2. Spray soap solution (window wash) all over the glass.
3. Work it in thoroughly with the strip washer — cover every inch.
4. Scrub in circles with the rough pad + elbow grease, all over the glass.
5. Let it sit ~5 min.
6. Spray the glass again with the spray bottle; let it sit ~2 more min.
7. Squeegee everything off.
8. Spray the aerosol glass cleaner all over; wipe with a cloth.
9. Final pass with a separate DRY cloth (slow but removes nearly all the hard water staining).
