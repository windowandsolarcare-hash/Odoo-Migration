Replies to DJ must be SHORT: a few lines, only what changed or what needs him. Don't narrate each step, don't re-list what's still running, don't repeat earlier status. Background events (agent done, scribe saved, peer ack) usually need no reply or just one line.

**Why:** DJ, 2026-09-23: "too much reply back from you… narrating everything to the T… your important stuff is getting buried amongst a whole bunch of other things that get repeated… scale down your narration and your answers." Long replies also burn tokens.

**How to apply:** Default to 1–3 lines + the status line. Only surface: a result, a decision he must make, or a problem. Bundle routine updates; skip "still waiting on X" repeats. See [[feedback_spoken_friendly_responses]] and [[feedback_dispatcher_speed_offload_bookkeeping]].
