**2026-07-24.** DJ's note "Create a goal-setting or priority app… timeline to say yes/no to requests… consider the obstacles, not just the date" → he wants to build a **Goal Board** in his app. Grew out of the note→tasks feature ([[project_note_to_tasks]]).

## What it is (from research: Gantt/roadmap/Kanban, Miro/Milanote whiteboard, WOOP)
NOT a full Gantt (too heavy on a phone). It's a **roadmap + WOOP + task grouping**:
- Goal (the "wish/outcome").
- **Milestones** on a vertical timeline, each with a target date + status.
- **Tasks** under each milestone (fed by note→tasks extraction).
- **Obstacles (WOOP** = Wish/Outcome/Obstacle/Plan, Gabriele Oettingen — DJ reinvented it): "If <obstacle> Then <when/then plan>". This is the differentiator vs a plain to-do app.
- Phase 3 (later): overlay his **job schedule** → real capacity → "can I say yes to this request?" (like Motion/Reclaim/Sunsama auto-scheduling).

## FORECAST — "will I make it?" (2026-07-24, DJ picked this as next step; commits goals ef67953, v2_goals bc1adfd)
goals_get now computes, per milestone: `work_left` (sum of incomplete tasks' allocated_hours) and a `forecast` {status: ontrack|behind|done|clear, proj: ISO date, weeks}. Driver: `wk_free` = avg over next 6 weeks of (40h − booked job hrs). CUMULATIVE — milestones consume free hours in order (ceil(work/wk_free) weeks each, accumulating), so proj dates cascade; behind = proj > deadline. Response adds `wk_free`. UI: `fcLine(m)` renders a colored line under each milestone head ("Xh of work left · On track/Behind · finish ~date"); goal-hero shows "~Wh free per week for goal work". ## Horizontal-overflow / sticky-broken fix (2026-07-24, commit 6515873)
DJ's phone showed the whole Goal Board shifted left + header not sticky. Cause: the long goal title in a `white-space:nowrap` span inside the flex `.head-t` had NO shrink constraint → it forced the header wider than the viewport → page overflowed sideways → sticky broke (position:sticky fails when an ancestor/page overflows horizontally). Fix: `.head-t{min-width:0;overflow:hidden}` + title span `flex:1;min-width:0;overflow:hidden;text-overflow:ellipsis;white-space:nowrap`. Verified in-browser at 390px: rowOverflow 0 (was 182), titleTruncates true, header sticky. ★ Lesson for any flex header with a nowrap title: the title's flex item MUST have `min-width:0` or it overflows on narrow screens.
