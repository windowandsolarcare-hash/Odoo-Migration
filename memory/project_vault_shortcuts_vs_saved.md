**DJ described his Vault mental model 2026-09-08 (folds into the Vault rework Phase-2 home redesign).** Two distinct kinds of items:

1. **SHORTCUTS** — **use Evernote's word "Shortcuts"; the current Vault "Reference / Reference Documents / Quick Reference" naming is NOT intuitive to DJ** (he said so directly). These = **frequently-referenced, quick-access FACTS: account information, addresses** — things he references fast + often and needs **instant, front-and-center**. This is the important one. (Maps to the existing `reference.py` "Quick Reference" cards feature — reframe it as Shortcuts.)
2. **JUST-SAVED docs** — things he saves because he wants them kept, **NOT** marked as a shortcut. General storage, not quick-access. ("I don't put them as references, I just save the doc.")

**Design implications (Phase-2 home redesign, alongside the search work):**
- Reframe "Quick Reference" → **"Shortcuts"** (his word), front-and-center, instant.
- Let DJ **mark an item as a shortcut vs just-save it** — the two behaviors are distinct in his head.
- The **instant fact-ranked search index** (Phase-2 search) IS the shortcut-retrieval engine — his shortcuts = exactly the "type a few chars, grab the fact" set. Home leads with Shortcuts. (This also ANSWERS the "what does DJ search for?" question: his references = account info + addresses.)

**★ Phase-3 privacy tie-in:** DJ's SHORTCUTS (account #s, addresses) ARE his personal/private data. So **"shortcuts → PRIVATE by default, never in Cheryl's shared Documents view"** is the clean default for the personal-vs-shared boundary when the Vault becomes the shared "Documents (W&SC)" surface. Build the note-index scopable so shortcuts can be excluded from Cheryl's view.
