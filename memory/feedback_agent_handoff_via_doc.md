**Why:** DJ is the relay between sessions, on a phone. Long chat text to copy is friction and can get mangled; a doc in `3_Documentation/` is versioned, readable by the other agent directly, and stays the single source of truth (matches [[feedback_no_re_listing]]).

**How to apply:** when handing work to the other agent: (1) write EVERYTHING — context, priority, rules, first deliverable, lane notes — into the brief doc itself (e.g. `3_Documentation/<NAME>_BRIEF.md`); (2) give DJ a paste paragraph of 1–2 sentences max: assignment name, priority, and the doc path. Nothing in the paragraph that isn't also in the doc.
