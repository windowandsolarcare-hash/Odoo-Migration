Context (2026-06-26): DJ hiring a tech (workers' comp now a cost) → re-examining pricing to cover all costs. "It's all about sales — charge enough to cover costs."

**THE QUOTE TOOL = `static/owner/quote.html`** (route `/owner/quote`, backend quotes.py). Pricing model is `LINE_ITEMS` (~line 456, comment "easy to edit if rates change"): per-pane, count × rate. **Current rates: Regular Panes $7 / Oversized $8 / Second-Story $9 / Regular Sliders $25 / Oversized Sliders $30 / Triple Sliders $35.** Plus a difficulty multiplier (Standard / Hard +15% / Very Hard +30%). Total = Σ(count × rate) × difficulty. Quote saves to an SO; can push to Workiz.

**PER-PANE is the chosen model (DJ's call) — and it auto-handles difficulty:** because you charge per PANE not per window, a picture window (1 pane) is automatically cheap (credit) and a 5-pane window is automatically 5× (ding). No separate rule needed. Beats square-footage (which can't see intricate/big/difficult windows). Picture-window convention: count as 1 REGULAR pane (not oversized) to give the credit.

**DJ's TARGET TOTALS by home size (the market sweet-spots he wants to hit):** 1,000–1,500 sf = ~$200; 1,500–2,200 sf = $200–300; 2,200–3,000 sf = $300–400; 3,000+ sf = $400–500. Anchor ~$200/hr for a 2-guy crew; average job ≈ 1 hr.

**PRICING MODEL DJ wants (cost-plus + capacity-based overhead recovery, from his printing-controller background):** Fixed/overhead costs ÷ (crew-hours × capacity% e.g. 70–80%) = overhead per billable hour; + direct labor per crew-hour (wages + workers' comp + payroll taxes); = break-even rate; + margin = price/hr; × job hours → per-job → calibrate per-pane. KEY: must cover ALL fixed costs even at 70% capacity (lower capacity ⇒ higher overhead/hr ⇒ price higher). Single-operation business ⇒ ONE blended hourly rate (no departmental/machine-hour allocation like printing's press-vs-binder). Cost model = the FLOOR; sweet-spots = market ceiling; price between, and the cost model must PROVE the sweet-spots clear the floor. TWO-PHASE PLAN: (1) build cost model from actuals — DJ wants this as an APP later (pull data from accounting + he enters/projects; "I don't want to write anything down"), NOT now; (2) over next 1–2 months quote EVERY job (count panes at end) to build pane data, then calibrate rates. NOTE: little pane-count data exists today.

**CURRENT RATES (APPLIED & LIVE 2026-06-26): $8 / $9 / $10.50 / $29 / $34.50 / $40.50** (reg/over/2nd-story panes, reg/over/triple sliders) = original +15% rounded to nearest 50¢ (~+14.7% blended).
