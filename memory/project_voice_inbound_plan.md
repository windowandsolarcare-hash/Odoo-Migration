**THE #1 PAIN to solve — caller heads-up.** In Workiz, his phone briefly flashes the caller, then the "call flow" NAME (Window&Solar Care / Google / Thumbtack call flow) COVERS IT UP and he can't tell who's calling. He badly wants to know WHO is calling. Solution stacks 3 layers (we control every layer once it's his Twilio + Render):
1. **Set caller ID on the forwarded leg = the ORIGINAL caller's number** (not the business/flow name) → his native phone shows the real caller (+ names them if saved).
2. **Private WHISPER before connect** (caller can't hear): "Call from {name}, {current customer/new}, on your {Thumbtack/Google/direct} line — press 1 to take it or hang up for voicemail." SOURCE is derived from WHICH of his numbers they dialed (5315=Thumbtack, 5350=GooglePPC, 5355=main, etc.) → keeps the call-flow's source value WITHOUT hiding the caller. Whisper also = screening + spam warning.
3. **App SCREEN-POP** (push within ~1-2s): Odoo lookup → card with name · address · last job · next job (same _find_customer the SMS inbox uses).

**Spam (he gets hammered, esp. toll-free + repeat numbers + vendors):** log every call → flag suspicious (toll-free prefixes 800/888/877/866/855/844/833, repeat non-customers, not in Odoo, optional reputation/Twilio Lookup). Whisper warns "unknown, possibly spam"; screen-pop shows 🚩 BLOCK button → one tap adds to blocklist (ir.config_parameter or res.partner tag) → future calls <Reject>ed before his phone rings. BLOCK-WITH-APPROVAL — no silent auto-block; DJ controls the list.

**RESOLVED 2026-06-24 — NO press-1, ever.** DJ's reality: dislikes answering; often physically CAN'T (working inside a customer's house); interacts with calls via his WATCH (glance → answer or dismiss). press-1-to-accept fights all of that (requires answering first, then keypad mid-call on a watch). So: (1) decision is VISUAL on the watch BEFORE answering (real caller-ID shown, not flow name); (2) the whisper becomes a NO-PRESS informational ANNOUNCE that plays only if he answers, then auto-connects ("call for W&SC from {name}, {current/new}, {source} line") — so he knows who even if he answered blind; (3) dismiss / can't-answer → voicemail (the WORKHORSE here, must be excellent — transcribe/push/log). Watch can only show a NUMBER unless caller is in his phone contacts → OPTIONAL enhancement: sync ~900 Odoo customers into his phone contacts (Google Contacts/CardDAV) so the watch shows the NAME natively; full name/addr/last+next job always in the app screen-pop card. Because he so often can't answer, the AI receptionist (answers + books when he can't) is the real payoff — voicemail catch-net is the bridge to it.

**DESKTOP SCREEN-POP = "the real magic" (DJ 2026-06-24).** When DJ is off the truck at his COMPUTER and the phone rings, he wants the customer's whole world to auto-throw onto his screen (classic call-center "screen pop" / agent-assist) — anticipating what they'll ask. TRIGGER: owner app open in a desktop browser tab holds a live channel (SSE/WebSocket/short-poll on Render); call hits Twilio → app pinged in real time → command-center pops automatically. Runs IN PARALLEL with the watch/voicemail flow (desktop open = big screen; on truck = watch). **CURRENT customer screen** (reuses existing assets: Customer brain [[project_customer_brain_job_actions]], shared scheduler [[project_shared_scheduler]] find_next_opening/build_day_plan, pricing x_studio_prices_per_service, analytics, comms log): identity/address/gate, full job history + last job (price/tech/notes/photos), next scheduled/next due date, balance + unpaid invoice + pay link, THEIR pricing per service, and the ANTICIPATION panel — run the scheduler LIVE → show next open dates in their area + one-tap book; AI BRIEF at top ("X, maintenance q3mo, last 3/12, next due 6/20, owes $0, likely calling to schedule, next opening Tue 9am"). **NEW/unknown caller screen (OPEN DESIGN Q — design w/ DJ):** reverse lookup (Twilio Lookup line-type/carrier/CNAM), city from area code, SOURCE from which # dialed (Thumbtack/Google/direct), service-area check (in area? y/n), spam flag, one-tap new-lead intake, pricing menu ready to quote. Shares ~everything with the AI receptionist (same lookup/scheduler/pricing brain, different output) → build screen-pop first, AI version reuses it. Constraints: needs a desktop tab OPEN+listening (can't pop on a computer that isn't connected); AI brief adds ~2-5s (render core instantly, stream the AI line); must render inside ~15-20s ring window.

**PRE-BAKED "call card" (DJ idea 2026-06-24, GOOD) — beat the ring-window latency.** Don't compute the screen-pop during the ~15-20s ring; PRE-BUILD a per-customer "call card" keyed by phone number and just LOAD it on inbound. TRIGGER = job finish / billing event (natural moment), refresh on key events (+ optional nightly). SPLIT for freshness (the key refinement): PRE-BAKE the slow+STABLE stuff (identity/address/gate, history, last job+photos, frequency, THEIR pricing, and especially the AI BRIEF / anticipated-asks narrative — the LLM call is the slow part, so baking it ahead is where the win is); COMPUTE LIVE the VOLATILE stuff that rots (next-open slot — schedule changes daily, must be current; current balance/unpaid invoice — they may have paid) — and those live bits are the FAST lookups anyway. It's a CACHE not the only path: keep a live-lookup fallback for callers with no fresh card (e.g. last job a year ago). Cheap wasted work (cards built for customers who never call) is worth the instant pop. Storage: ir.config_parameter keyed by normalized phone (or res.partner field/attachment).

**CARRIER-VM FIX (2026-06-27) — the big one.** First version used a plain `<Dial><Number>` to DJ's cell; when DJ didn't answer, his CARRIER voicemail answered the forwarded leg → Twilio marked it "completed" → caller landed in DJ's PERSONAL voicemail (DJ heard a "subscriber, press pound" telco prompt). DJ wants voicemail 100% IN-APP, no personal VM, and NO key-press. REBUILT to conference + answering-machine detection: `/voice/incoming` plays greeting then holds the caller in a per-call `<Conference room-{CallSid}>` (startConferenceOnEnter=false) AND fires a SEPARATE outbound REST call to DJ's cell with `MachineDetection=Enable` (`_dial_dj`). `/voice/dj-bridge` reads `AnsweredBy`: **human** → whisper + join the conference (startConferenceOnEnter=true, endConferenceOnExit=true); **anything else (machine/fax/carrier-VM)** → `<Hangup/>` that leg. `/voice/dj-status` (DJ leg completed, StatusCallbackEvent=completed): if a human connected → nothing; else → REST-redirect the caller's call to `/voice/voicemail-redirect` → OUR `<Record>`. So caller can NEVER hit DJ's personal VM. Tradeoff vs the old simple Dial: REST-created outbound legs can't spoof arbitrary caller ID, so DJ's phone shows the BUSINESS line, not the real caller's number — the no-press WHISPER ("Call for Window and Solar Care from {name}") carries the who-is-calling instead (native caller-ID-on-watch would need the screening/press approach DJ rejected, or contact-sync). AMD adds ~3-5s + costs; treats only AnsweredBy=='human' as connect.
