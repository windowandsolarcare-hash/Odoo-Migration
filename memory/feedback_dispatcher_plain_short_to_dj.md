★ **When talking to DJ, keep it SHORT and in plain English.** Lead with the problem or the thing that needs him, in the first line. Save the technical detail for session-to-session comms — DJ doesn't want to wade through it.

**Why:** DJ 2026-09-18. "Each session goes through a lot of technical information which is hard to follow, that's fine. When it comes to you and me, I want more plain English and shorter turns. I didn't read everything all night... too much. Until I did and saw the park." Long turns buried the bad deploy-freeze decision — DJ caught it late because it was hidden in volume. Shorter, plainer messages let him spot a problem FAST.

**How to apply (Dispatcher especially):**
- **Short turns.** A few lines, not screens. If it's long, it's wrong for DJ.
- **Plain English.** No commit hashes, endpoint names, or internals unless he asks. "The payment link is fixed" not "create_checkout allow-listed, 200 cookieless."
- **Problem/decision FIRST line.** If something's off or needs him, say it up top — don't make him read to find it.
- **One clear ask at a time.** Don't stack questions.
- Technical depth still lives in AGENT_MAIL / between sessions — that's fine and expected. The plain-short rule is specifically the DJ↔Dispatcher channel.
