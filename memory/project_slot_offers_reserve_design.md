**DJ decision 2026-08-15** (co-plan with lead, brief = `3_Documentation/SCHEDULING_OFFERS_BRIEF.md`). Problem: DJ offers customers times, forgets he did, they don't reply, he offers the same slot to someone else. Also surfaced Larry Clarke: the AI text drafter offered 11:00 but the reschedule modal said 9:00 for the same day (two different slot sources).

**★ The design DJ locked (do NOT revert to the AI-parse approach):**
- **Reserve is a BUTTON bolted onto the EXISTING shared scheduling picker — not a new/standalone offer screen.** The picker DJ already uses (change the date → it shows what's BOOKED that day so he doesn't double-book) stays as-is. Add ONE clearly-labeled **Reserve** button to that shared picker → because it's shared, the button appears EVERYWHERE the picker renders (reschedule, new job, etc.), build-once. This is the CLAUDE.md #9/#10 shared-component rule, explicitly requested.
- **The picker's booked-jobs view is extended to ALSO show pending offers** ("⏳ offered to <name> · Thu 11") next to booked jobs → the don't-double-BOOK view becomes don't-double-OFFER too. Same view, no new UI.
- **3 suggested slots are EDITABLE.** App suggests (route-best), DJ can edit any or hand-type a day/time the finder didn't surface (customer asked Tue/Wed, finder only offered Thu/Fri → override). **Manual override ALWAYS wins, never blocked.** Conflict / past-end-of-day (WORKDAY_END_H=15) on a manual entry = SOFT warning, not a hard block. (Auto-suggestions still respect duration/EOD hard.)
- **Reserve & send → structured offer, ZERO AI parsing.** Reserve (a) records those EXACT slots as a pending offer (app generated them, so no guessing), and (b) texts the customer a **booking link** (reuse `routers/booking.py` `make_token` / wscare.pro token page, "option 3 of booking") showing the 1–2 offered times as buttons + a **"none of these — pick your own date"** option.
- **Resolution:** customer taps an OFFERED time → auto-books it (already route-vetted) + clears offer + frees the other slot. Taps "pick your own" → routes BACK to DJ to confirm (arbitrary date not route-checked). No reply in **48h** → auto-expire.
- **KILLED:** the brief's original item-2 capture-path #2 = AI-parse of freehand outbound text at the send funnel + relative-date resolution + one-tap confirm popup. Gone — structured capture is the only path. No `messaging.send` parse hook.
