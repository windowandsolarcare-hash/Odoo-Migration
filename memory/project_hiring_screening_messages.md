## Screening Message (send immediately after application)

> Hi [Name], thanks for applying to the Window Cleaner position at Window & Solar Care. A few quick questions before we move forward:
> - Do you have a valid driver's license?
> - Are you comfortable working on ladders at heights up to 1 story?
> - Are you looking for part-time hours to start (20–30 hrs/week) with potential to grow full-time?
> — Dan, Window & Solar Care

**Rules:**
- No promises of a call, no timeline commitments, no "before we set up a call"
- Heights = 1 story (not 2 — too intimidating, most CV homes are single-story)
- Brand name = "Window & Solar Care" — never "A Window and Solar Care"

---

## Graceful Rejection (for candidates not moving to phone screen)

> Hi [Name], thank you for taking the time to answer my questions — I appreciate it. We received a much higher volume of applications than expected and have already moved forward with a small group of candidates for this round. I'll keep your information on file and will reach out if something opens up. Thanks again for your interest in Window & Solar Care.
> — Dan

**Rules:**
- Send manually through Indeed chat — do NOT use Indeed's automated rejection button
- Never say "rejected" or "not moving forward"
- "This round" language keeps door open for future hires
- Coachella Valley is small — these people are neighbors and potential customers

---

## Why the original message was a problem

The first version said "Before we set up a call" — this created an implicit promise that answering the 3 questions would result in a call. ~60 candidates answered in good faith. Most did not get a call. DJ was accountable for not catching it before sending. Lesson: never make commitments in screening messages.
