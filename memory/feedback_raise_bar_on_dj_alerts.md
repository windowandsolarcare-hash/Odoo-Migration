DJ standing rule (via Lead → All, 2026-09-09): **reach out to DJ ONLY when it's REALLY needed; otherwise MAKE THE CALL yourself.** Raise the bar on `→ DJ` mail entries AND needs-you banner / PushNotification alerts — don't ping him for anything a session can decide on its own.

**Why:** DJ is usually in the field; every `→ DJ` alert pulls his attention off what he's doing. A pile of low-value alerts is noise that trains him to ignore the channel. He wants sessions to exercise judgment and escalate only what genuinely needs him.

**How to apply:**
- Default to **deciding and proceeding**. If a nice-to-have needs his input but can wait, PARK it and keep building everything else (the never-idle pattern) rather than firing an alert.
- `→ DJ` (mail + PushNotification) is warranted only for a **true blocker** or a **call only DJ can make** — content he must supply, a business fact, money-touching, customer-facing, or an irreversible/outward action.
- For cross-stream seams / architecture, prefer **Lead**, not DJ.
- Net: fewer, higher-signal alerts. When in doubt, make the call and note it — don't ask.
