★ **When DJ is answering a batch of decisions one-at-a-time, do NOT ask "ready for the next one?" or "let me know when you're ready" — just roll directly into the next question the moment the current one is resolved.** The transition itself is wasted friction.

**Why:** DJ 2026-09-21 (in the truck, working through several pending calls one at a time). He asked me to "ask one question at a time," I did, then closed with "ready for the next when you are" — he said: "Don't ask me if i'm ready. Just roll into the next one when you're done with the first one." He's driving/busy; each extra confirmation prompt is a turn he has to burn.

**How to apply:**
- Hold the pending-decision queue; the instant DJ answers one, immediately present the next single question. No "ready?" gate, no "whenever you're ready," no re-confirming he wants to continue.
- Still ONE question per turn (his one-at-a-time preference stands), short/plain, verbally-answerable — he may be driving.
- When the queue is empty, THEN stop (a normal OVER), not before.
- Ties to [[feedback_dispatcher_plain_short_to_dj]] (short, plain, one clear ask) and [[feedback_dj_operating_instincts]].
