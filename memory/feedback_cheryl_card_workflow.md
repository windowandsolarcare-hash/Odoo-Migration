DJ set + refined this 2026-09-28. The standing procedure when Design works with **Cheryl** on a card:

## The IMAGE loop (Cheryl generates in Midjourney; Design writes the prompt)
1. **Cheryl initiates:** she asks Design to come up with a **Midjourney prompt**.
2. **Brainstorm the image together** — kick the idea back and forth (what they want Midjourney to create) until they agree on the shot.
3. **Design WRITES the Midjourney prompt** (core Design job — [[project_wsc_print_build_pipeline]] logo/type rules still apply; never let MJ render text/logo/phone). Steer negative space for the copy.
4. **Design gives Cheryl the prompt + plain instructions:** paste it into Midjourney; on the image she chooses, press **Upscale → "Subtle"** (MJ's own subtle upscale on the raw photo — expected/good, NOT the banned Upscayl-on-the-flattened-composite step); then **download** it.
5. **Design picks the image up from her DOWNLOADS folder** and moves forward. (Design does NOT need to drive her logged-in Midjourney — the controlled browser is a separate session and can't inherit her MJ login; confirmed 2026-09-28.)

## The COPY (COLLABORATE — do NOT unilaterally author)
- **It is NOT Design's job to write/"copyright" the copy UNLESS Cheryl asks.** Design's job is to **coordinate + collaborate with Cheryl on developing the copy** — Cheryl drives the content; Design helps shape it. Only write it outright if she asks Design to.
- **Design IS the designer of the copy:** once the words are agreed, Design typesets/lays them out on the canvas (the artifact) as real type, front AND back.

## Deliverable
- Build both sides to exact print spec; if mailed EDDM apply [[project_eddm_mailing_rules]] (size 9×6.5, address top-half, indicia) + the [[project_wsc_print_build_pipeline]].
- **Present a PROOF to Cheryl** — the RENDERED card WITH trim + safe (+ EDDM) guide lines, as an image she can see — in plain, non-technical language ([[feedback_render_design_before_presenting]], [[feedback_design_end_to_end_workflow]]).
- **One stable link**, edit by conversation to the SAME artifact URL. Cheryl is non-technical — talking to Design IS the interface; never make her learn a tool.

**Why:** DJ split it so Cheryl only does the creative image part in a tool she knows (Midjourney) and drives her own message; Design carries the prompt-writing, the layout/typesetting, the print spec, and the proofing — where the real mistakes live.

**How to apply:** likely business = **Cheryl Johnson REALTOR®** (company 2). Front and back are BOTH Design's design deliverable — never just the front. This corrects an earlier draft of this rule that wrongly said "Design writes the copy."
