**PART 1 (priority) — "Send scheduling options" 3-choice.** Launch button on the **v2_field job-detail** screen → texts the customer a proposed date + a `wscare.pro` tokened link → **portal-rich** customer page (branded, greet by name, show address/job type/history via booking.py `make_token` + `/api/me?token=`).
