DJ 2026-10-03, on the hiring reach-out messages: **do NOT word them so the candidate thinks they've already got it.** Avoid "you're a perfect match / strong match," "exactly what we're looking for," "exactly the reliability this job needs," and similar. DJ hasn't decided who moves to the interview step, and an over-promising first message makes a later pass feel like a blindside.

**Why:** retention-first hiring means being deliberate and talking to several people; the first reach-out's job is to test genuine interest (require a reply), NOT to court or commit. Honest expectation-setting up front = no hurt feelings when he doesn't pick someone.

**How to apply:** calibrate first reach-outs to the handoff's locked tone — "**looks like a possible fit**" + a line that signals we're **talking with a few people** + the experience/what-are-you-looking-for question. A light FACTUAL nod to their background is fine (e.g. "your driving experience and Class A license"), but never a verdict or promise. Keep it warm but non-committal. (The already-sent Tier A reach-outs used warmer wording — can't unsend, but recalibrate from Tier B onward.) Ties to [[feedback_dj_operating_instincts]] and [[project_ziprecruiter_lead_hiring]].
